# Morph — Take the Shape and Powers of Any Mob You Defeat

Morph 9minecraft Mod lets you transform into almost any creature in the game just by defeating them. When you take down a mob, you collect its shape and can switch into that form whenever you want. This mod gives you the exact look, size, and special abilities of the creatures you collect, completely changing how you move around your world.

Shape Shifting: Turn into any mob you defeat and add it to your personal collection of disguises.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **To_Craft**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
